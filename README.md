# benn_cmif
CMIF-files of published correspondence pieces of Benn, Gottfried (1886–1956)

This repository consists of CMIF-files of published correspondences of Benn, Gottfried.

In case you wonder what a CMIF-file is, CMIF stands for Correspondence Metadata Interchange format and the documentation can be found here: https://correspsearch.net/en/documentation.html

And if you desire a search interface, please use: https://correspsearch.net/de/suche.html?s=https://d-nb.info/gnd/118509047

In case you ask yourself who Benn, Gottfried was, this might get you started: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Benn

A bibliography of his correspondences in print is accessible in this Zotero group: tba

Over 800 correspondence pieces from two major correspondences of Benn will be immediately accessible here: https://edview.dla-marbach.de/
